## Reddit (r/ClaudeAI, r/SideProject, r/indiehackers)

**Title:** I built a go-to-market plugin for Claude Code that plans and executes product launches from the terminal

I've been building a set of Claude Code plugins as a "compound startup toolkit" — tools that work together to go from idea to shipped product without leaving the terminal.

The latest piece is plugin-gtm, a go-to-market engine. Here's what it does:

- **/gtm-analyze** — scans your project (README, package.json, source code, git history) and builds a structured product profile: what it does, who it's for, key differentiators
- **/gtm-plan** — walks you through positioning, messaging, ideal customer profile, distribution channels, pricing strategy, and a phased launch timeline with auto-generated checklist
- **/gtm-content** — generates launch content (landing pages, README, emails, social posts, blog posts) based on your plan so everything stays consistent
- **/gtm-research** — competitive analysis and market research via web search, with findings that feed back into your plan

Under the hood it's an MCP server with 20 tools and SQLite persistence, so your GTM state survives across sessions. It also ships with 4 blueprint stubs for plugin-hive (our multi-agent orchestration plugin) so you can run complex GTM workflows like a full launch or content sprint as automated agent swarms.

The fun part: I used plugin-gtm to plan its own launch. The product analysis, GTM plan, README, and these social posts were all generated through its workflows.

Install: `claude plugin install https://github.com/twofoldtech-dakota/plugin-gtm.git`

Repo: github.com/twofoldtech-dakota/plugin-gtm

It's part of a trilogy:
- plugin-architect (design & build)
- plugin-hive (multi-agent orchestration)
- plugin-gtm (go to market)

Open source, MIT licensed, Node.js 22. Happy to answer questions.
